## F1. THEME CLAIMS ON REAL EARNINGS TEXT ARE PLEASANTRIES (DRIVES CHG-009)

| Case | Result with your pipeline and the stub gateway |
|---|---|
| RBC FY2026 Q3, topic Net Interest Income & Margins | 20 topic-tagged passages, 0 quarantined, only 4 candidate sentences, 1 claim: [redacted for public repository: transcript excerpt] |
| TD FY2026 Q3, topic Credit Quality & Provisions | 17 passages, 4 candidate sentences, 1 claim: [redacted for public repository: transcript excerpt] |
| RBC FY2026 Q2 (unified generation), topic Net Interest Income & Margins | 5 passages, 4 candidate sentences, 1 claim: [redacted for public repository: transcript excerpt] |

Cause: `candidates_for` keeps the FIRST four sentences per topic and source in document order, and real calls open with greetings and hand-offs. A real model would also only ever see those four sentences, so even a good model cannot write a useful theme. The code is not wrong for your synthetic fixtures; the fixtures simply have no greetings. Also: 24 candidates is a very small window for a call with hundreds of sentences.

## F2. DOCUMENT RETRIEVAL RETURNS NOTHING FOR TAXONOMY LABELS, AND RANKS BY PAGE ORDER (DRIVES CHG-010)

| Source and case | topic = 'Net Interest Income & Margins' | topic = 'net interest margin' | topic = 'credit' |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slides RBC FY2026 Q3 | 0 hits | 7 hits | 8 hits |
| Slides BNS FY2026 Q3 | 0 hits | 8 hits | 8 hits |
| RTS RBC FY2026 Q3 | 0 hits | 1 hit | 8 hits |
| RTS TD FY2026 Q2 | 0 hits | 8 hits | 8 hits |

Earnings uses a taxonomy of topic labels (the classification_names list), but slide and RTS text never contains those labels, so a substring match on them finds nothing. When a plain word does match, the 8 results are chosen by file and page order (ORDER BY hit, then distance NULLS LAST, then page), not by relevance; for 'credit' the first eight chunks in the document win.
