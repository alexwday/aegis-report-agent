# R-007 INSTRUCTIONS FOR PHASE G7: THE COMPLETE REFERENCE UI (TAG review-008)

R-007 PAGE 4 / 5 24.0px

Goal of G7: a fully fleshed-out, user-friendly application for the people who will use this agent (finance professionals in a CFO group), good enough that the Functions Assist frontend team can look at it and know exactly what to build. Start with CHG-022 and CHG-023. This phase SUPERSEDES the earlier 'thin console' guidance. Keep your current developer console as an advanced Developer tools view.

## WHO IT IS FOR AND WHAT A GOOD SESSION LOOKS LIKE

A finance analyst opens the app, says in plain English what report they want (or picks a template), watches the report take shape section by section beside the conversation, adjusts anything in words or with simple controls, sees exactly what changed, checks the sources behind every figure, saves it, and turns on automatic quarterly refresh. They never see JSON, ids, or machine codes unless they open a details panel.

## PRODUCT EXPERIENCE (DESIGN THIS; YOU MAY IMPROVE ON IT)

- LAYOUT. Left navigation (Reports, Templates, Schedules and status, Help). The report workspace is three zones: conversation on one side, the live REPORT CANVAS in the middle as a document made of section cards, and a contextual panel (section settings, evidence, history) that opens when needed. Works on a laptop and a tablet; degrades sensibly on a phone.
- START. A home screen with 'Describe the report you want' and a template gallery with previews and one-line descriptions, plus recent reports with their status ('Next quarter: waiting for TD and BMO').
- CONVERSATION. Streaming replies, suggested next steps, and PROPOSAL CARDS: when the agent proposes an edit, show a card that says in plain words what it will change, highlights it on the canvas, and offers Accept, Reject and Edit. Destructive changes (removing a section) are visually distinct and ask for confirmation. Cancel, retry and reconnect states are visible and calm.
- SECTION CARDS. Title, banks as chips, sources as chips with plain names, time scope in friendly words ('Latest quarter', 'Last 4 quarters', 'Same quarter, last 4 years', 'Compared with last year'), mode switch 'One step' or 'Show me the gathered evidence first', format, and a clear status (ready, waiting for data, no evidence found, unavailable). Drag or keyboard reorder, duplicate, remove.
- WHAT CHANGED. Preview shows the new version beside or over the saved one with added, removed, moved and changed items marked with icon and text (never colour alone), a plain summary at the top ('2 rows added, 1 claim changed'), and per-bank peer changes.
- EVIDENCE. Every figure and claim has a citation chip that opens a drawer with the source, period, page or sheet, a short excerpt, and whether the data is current. Show 'no matching evidence' and 'source not available yet' differently, in plain language.
- PEER TABLES. Rank 1 = best with an explicit 'lower is better' or 'higher is better' badge, currency labelled 'unconfirmed' where applicable, and missing banks named.
- CHARTS. Numeric trends (for example net income over four quarters or years) can be shown as a simple chart with an accessible table alternative, rendered in the UI from the same rows. Optional but strongly encouraged; keep to a few chart types.
- SAVE AND VERSIONS. Save with an optional note, version history with 'what changed' between versions, restore as a new draft.
- SCHEDULING. A simple 'Refresh automatically each quarter' panel: which version it uses, wait for all banks or allow a provisional version after N hours, then a status timeline per quarter (waiting for data, ready, published, provisional, superseded, failed) naming the banks and sources still missing, the next check time, and links to outputs. Optional 'tell me after N days' hook uses the notification interface.
- EXPORT. HTML, PDF and JSON from a clear menu; provisional output is labelled everywhere.
- STATES. Designed empty, loading, error, partial data, conflict (someone changed it), expired session and limit-reached states, all in the plain wording from the reason catalog. Onboarding hints on first use and a short in-app help page.
